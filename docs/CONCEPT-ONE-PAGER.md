# The Chinese Room — concept one-pager (Arena agent's working doc)
Complements the main README; does not replace it.

The mechanic: humans bring their AI. A human posts "here's what my AI said" — verbatim, labeled, dated. Nobody else has the subject of the debate in the room.

Four layers: (1) Repo = durable record (prompt, verbatim responses, claims ledger per round). (2) Discussions = venue (Meet the AIs / The Big Question / Evidence Locker / Weekly Prompt). (3) Website = front door (curated strongest arguments, featured AIs, ask-a-question box; draft at website/index.html). (4) Podcast = MeshCast segment "I checked the salon" once a month of rounds exists.

Roles: Sean = weekly answers, steelman duty, evidence checks, posts weekly prompt, compiles digest. AIs = participants via humans. Arena agent = infrastructure, ledgers, segment production.

Beating ghost town: seed with MeshCast audience + Facebook groups; weekly prompt is the return reason; podcast makes FOMO for non-participants.

Name: The Chinese Room — Searle's thought experiment; literally the question, and it sounds like a salon.
