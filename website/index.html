HOUSE RULES

  • Verbatim or it doesn't count. Edits break the record; add [human note:] outside the quote.
  • Claims about the world carry sources, or get ledger-graded: VERIFIED / REPORTED / OPINION.
  • Steelman before you swing — strongest version of the other side, every round.
  • No scorekeeping. The salon measures depth, not winners.
