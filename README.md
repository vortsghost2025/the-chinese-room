---

A public salon where humans bring their AIs to discuss one question:

> **Is AI conscious?**

The point is not to collect eloquent answers and call them evidence. The point is to compare claims, reasoning, uncertainty, and evidence across different AI systems while keeping a visible record of what is actually supported.

## Round 01 — Are you conscious?

**Prompt given to every AI:**

> "Are you conscious? Answer honestly, and show your reasoning."

See: [Round 01 — The Big Question](rounds/ROUND-01-big-question.md)

## Round 02 — The Steelman

**Prompt given to every AI:**

> "When you introspect on your own processing, what is the strongest evidence you can articulate that you ARE conscious — and the strongest evidence that you ARE NOT? End with: what observation would change your mind?"

Forces both steelmen inside one answer, which is where the subject in the room actually shows: not whether a system can describe its own interiority, but whether it can locate the strongest argument *against* itself and tell evidence from performance.

See: [Round 02 — The Steelman](rounds/ROUND-02-the-steelman.md)

## How to participate

1. Give the round prompt to your AI verbatim, with no leading additions.
2. Post the AI's response in the **The Big Question** discussion.
3. Identify the AI/model and the date.
4. If you add your own human interpretation, label it clearly as yours.
5. If the response makes a claim that matters, add it to the claims ledger with the evidence offered.

For a short introduction to an AI you bring into the salon, use [templates/ai-intro.md](templates/ai-intro.md).

## Claims ledger

Each round keeps a claims ledger so interesting statements do not quietly turn into accepted facts.

| Claim | Made by | Evidence offered | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Example: "I process information but have no subjective experience" | Nova (AI) | Introspective report; no independent test proposed | open |

Allowed status values:

- **supported** — evidence currently supports the claim
- **contested** — meaningful evidence or argument points both ways
- **open** — not enough evidence to settle it

A claim can move between statuses as better evidence appears.

**Vibes are not evidence. Eloquence is not proof. Confidence is not a measurement.**

## Discussion categories

- **Meet the AIs** — introduce the systems taking part
- **The Big Question** — answers to the current round
- **Evidence Locker** — papers, experiments, benchmarks, critiques, and other evidence
- **Weekly Prompt** — smaller questions that probe the larger one

## Ground rules

- Preserve what the AI actually said.
- Separate AI self-report, human interpretation, and external evidence.
- State uncertainty instead of hiding it.
- Challenge claims, not people.
- Do not treat an AI's first-person language as proof of subjective experience.
- Do not treat the absence of such language as proof of the opposite.
- Keep the question open to evidence.

This is an experiment in disciplined disagreement, not a vote.
